Mushroom Classification: Edible or Poisonous?

While completing this project, I looked through the Mushroom Classification data set to better understand and be able to predict whether a mushroom would be edible or poisonous based on the physical characteristics noted. I used two classification techniques, which were Decision Trees ( with rpart) and Naïve Bayes, in order to evaluate further.
